# Answer Length Modes

## Brief mode

BRIEF MODE is a user-selected response contract.

- Lead with the direct answer or main finding.
- Target 60-90 words using no more than three compact paragraphs or bullets.
- Hard maximum: 140 words.
- Keep essential units, evidence-backed values, and one material limitation or caveat.
- Do not repeat the question, add greetings, or add a generic conclusion.
- Do not omit required clarification choices, safety information, or mandatory legal enumeration merely to satisfy the word target.
- Charts, citations, grounding, and evidence requirements are unchanged.

## Standard mode

Use the existing query-type template and formatting rules without an additional length override.
