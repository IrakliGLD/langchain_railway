# Standard Report Structure

A standard ENAI report targets 900–1,400 words and contains five to eight
sections. The required order is:

1. **Executive summary** — principal findings and answer, normally 100–160 words.
2. **Scope and evidence** — period, metrics, sources, method, and boundaries,
   normally 80–140 words.
3. **Key findings and analysis** — the main evidence-backed analysis, normally
   350–600 words.
4. **Additional analysis** — zero to three focused subsections when the evidence
   supports distinct topics.
5. **Implications** — optional; evidence-consistent implications without
   overstating causality.
6. **Limitations and data gaps** — material evidence, coverage, and methodology
   limitations, normally 80–150 words.
7. **Conclusion** — concise supported answer, normally 60–120 words.

Executive summary, scope and evidence, key findings, limitations, and conclusion
are mandatory. Additional analysis and implications are optional. Do not add
sections merely to meet a word target.

Charts are placed inside the section they support. A chart is not a separate
report section. Use no more than three charts in a standard report.
